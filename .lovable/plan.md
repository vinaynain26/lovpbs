
Here are a few design suggestions for the Startup Portfolio widget:

### Option A: Grid Gallery Instead of Carousel
Replace the horizontal auto-scroll carousel with a **static grid** (e.g., 4-5 columns) showing founder photos with the startup name overlaid. Clicking/hovering reveals the tagline and tags. This is more editorial, reduces visual noise, and lets users scan all startups at once.

### Option B: Featured Spotlight + Compact Grid
Show **one large featured startup card** (rotating or static) on the left with full details, and a **compact logo grid** of all other startups on the right. This gives depth to one story while keeping the rest visible.

### Option C: Magazine-Style Masonry Layout
Use a **masonry/bento grid** with varying card sizes — 2-3 hero-sized cards for top startups (EIGHT, Bullspree, Beyond Veda) and smaller tiles for the rest. This creates visual hierarchy and breaks the monotony of equal-sized cards.

### Option D: Stats Banner + Scrollable Cards (Cleaner Current)
Keep the carousel but **reduce card height** (remove description text, show only founder image + tagline + logo). Add **clear section breaks** — a full-width dark divider and distinct heading treatment between Portfolio and Shark Tank.

### For Shark Tank Section Specifically:
- Move it into its **own distinct visual block** with a darker/lighter background shift
- Or collapse it into a **horizontal logo strip** with "As seen on Shark Tank India" branding — clicking reveals details
